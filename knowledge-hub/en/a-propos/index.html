About

Hugo
Lahutte

Passionate about audio, image and everything that stirs emotion. 17 years growing Son-Vidéo.com, now CEO of Cobra, publisher of Haute Fidélité and founder of HL Consulting — and I now build almost all of my tools and my content with Claude: a publishing ERP, agents, an action queue, a content machine, a private brain. Without being a developer. In public.

My background

It all starts with a passion: music and film. As a student, I sell electronics at Darty, then dip into events and Japanese culture by helping organize Japan Expo for five years — stage manager of the main hall, logistics, sound. You learn to adapt to anything.

Then comes the defining adventure: 16 years at Son-Vidéo.com, France's number one online retailer for hi-fi and home theater. I touch everything — purchasing, sales, commercial strategy, marketing, advertising (on- and offline), brand creation and import. Head of purchasing, then of marketing communication, then of commercial strategy, then of partnerships. That's where I learn to take a company from €10M to €62M, and where I build a network across the whole industry.

What drives me

A self-confessed geek, in love with music and film, sensitive to design and beautiful objects — my desk looks like my interests (Star Wars, Dune, vinyl, great speakers…). That universe directly feeds HiFi Lovers.

Today, what drives me is passing it on: showing how AI concretely changes a team's daily life, and helping as many people as possible take hold of it — no jargon, with real cases and real numbers. That's the whole point of this Knowledge Hub, of my method (rewritten in September 2026) and of the guides. And since this summer, of a conviction: what you know has to live in a Brain that all your AIs can read — a stale brain is worse than an empty brain.
