How I use Claude

Read the full guide →

The core of my method comes down to a few .md files I drop into Claude's "Project Instructions." A main CLAUDE.md that defines my tone, my rules, and my context — plus extension files loaded on demand (strategy, dev…) — and a dedicated file for each brand's DNA.

The guiding principle: Compound Engineering. Every time Claude gets my style wrong, I add a rule. The system gets better with use — and all future work benefits.

  • 2026-08-28

    AI doesn't just code my request — it verifies its own assumptions

    Odoo change requests were coming in as one-liners — no location, no click behavior, no "what must not change." An internal pipeline now frames them properly: a guided form (8 fields, voice input supported), a place to upload screenshots and an auto-transcribed video, then a two-pass AI review — it asks structured questions, the server checks them against the real Odoo database in read-only mode, and the AI concludes. It reclassified a dev request as ten minutes of configuration, and spotted that one proposed solution didn't actually eliminate the supplier calls it claimed to avoid. ~100 s and a few cents per review.

    HiFi Watch: the daily bot repaired, expanded (30 → 64 sources), and made reliable

    The daily HiFi watch bot (Telegram) had been broken since June 15 — the hardcoded AI model had reached end of life. Fixed (model now selected automatically, so a future update can't break the watch), significantly expanded (30 → 64 sources: FR specialist and mainstream press, Europe, Reddit, Head-Fi/diyAudio forums, YouTube), and made more reliable (HTML delivery, deduplication, alert if a source dies for 3 days, ~1-month memory, capped cost of ~€3–4/month). Validated in production: 125 articles → 123 topics → 4 Telegram messages with no errors.

  • 2026-08-07

    Price tracking & automated competitive alignment

    Manually monitoring hundreds of SKUs against Darty, Boulanger, Fnac, or Son-Vidéo wasn't realistic. A rules engine inside Odoo (cobra_price_rules) reads competitor prices delivered by Wiser several times a day and calculates a target price — best valid competitor, bounded by a minimum margin. Two modes: observation (simulates) then live (applies and pushes to Shopify), with going live remaining a human decision. Guardrails: never sell at a loss, absurd offers (below 60% of our price) are ignored, pair/unit normalization. 6 product families live, ~300 prices realigned; the rest adjusts automatically on each Wiser update.

    Claude · Cobra
  • 2026-08-07

    An app that reads supplier price lists so I don't have to

    Every supplier price list (PDF or "new product" email) used to require manual handling: read it, find the product in Odoo, verify EAN/SKU, enter purchase and sale prices. An internal app ("Product Update & Creation") automates the pipeline: upload a PDF or monitor a read-only mailbox, extract EAN/SKU/prices via a Claude model, match against the Odoo database, detect duplicates before creation, systematic human validation, and full traceability. Intentional guardrail: no newly created product is published online automatically.

  • 2026-08-07

    Discovering a pricing system overhaul by testing it

    A per-variant pricing system overhaul had been in preparation for weeks. While testing the price update app, a discrepancy between the price written in Odoo and the price synced on the storefront revealed the overhaul was already live — without any formal confirmation. Each variant now has its own sale price, eliminating the risk of price contamination between variants. The app was updated to write to the new field, and the issue was escalated to the ERP vendor. Lesson: test in the real world rather than trusting the docs.

  • 2026-07-13

    Customer order status: invoiced, paid, delivered — three badges instead of one

    Nothing clearly distinguished "the invoice went out," "the customer paid," and "the goods were delivered." Three independent badges (Invoicing / Payment / Delivery) replace the merged status: color coding, deposit percentage displayed, automatic toggle from balance due → remaining to ship, delivered lines grayed out. Ported from the supplier module already in place; two latent calculation bugs fixed in the process.

  • 2026-07-12

    Overhauling the Odoo pricing system (cobra_price): one single source of truth

    The Odoo pricing model was layered and fragile: an auto-calculated web pricelist, stacked price lists, a hardcoded 20% VAT, and zero margin the moment a product went out of stock. A single cobra_price field becomes THE source of truth via a _price_compute override — cost and margin recalculated, Omnibus-compliant strikethrough prices, immediate Shopify sync. 6 modules touched, ~7,300 products migrated, with real post-production monitoring (a display bug fixed the same day, 492 products on promotion pushed back).
