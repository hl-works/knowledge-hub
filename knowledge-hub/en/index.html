New · September 2026

Loose ideas at the end of the week. A Growth Hub running in production by Tuesday.

There was no specification. Just an intuition: stop stacking dashboards and build a tool that says what to do next, with the right evidence. I worked with ChatGPT and Codex over the weekend. By Tuesday, Growth was already running in production.

What started as an app produced much more: several repositories and components created or restructured, a reliable Data and BigQuery platform, a deployed application with tests and guardrails, and an agentic core ready to be instantiated for other projects. Not a mock-up: a system connected to real sources, designed to recommend and then measure actions.

HL OS: one foundation, five building blocks, and one rule — AI proposes, the human validates.

How I use Claude

Read the full guide →

Context is everything, not the prompt. My method organizes what Claude knows into two layers: the CLAUDE.md files, versioned in my repos (“how to work with me”: tone, principles, hard rules, per-domain extensions), and the Brain, a private wiki in OKF format (“what we know”: entities, projects, decisions, method, notes). Three doors — Chat writes, Cowork delegates, Code executes — two agents, skills for procedures, connectors for tools.

The loop that makes it all grow: every mistake becomes a rule, every session becomes a capture. And one golden rule, from the smallest script to my systems: AI proposes, the human validates. Fully rewritten in September 2026, starting prompt included.

  • 2026-08-28

    AI doesn't code my request — it verifies what it assumes

    Odoo change requests used to arrive as a one-liner — no where, no what-happens-on-click, no “what must not move.” An internal chain frames them: a guided form (8 fields, dictation possible), screenshot and auto-transcribed video upload, then a two-pass AI review — it asks structured questions, the server checks them against the real Odoo database in read-only mode, the AI concludes. It reclassified a dev request as ten minutes of configuration, and saw that a solution didn't remove the supplier calls it claimed to avoid. ~100 s and a few cents per review.

  • 2026-08-07

    Price tracking & automatic competitive alignment

    We couldn't manually watch hundreds of references against Darty, Boulanger, Fnac or Son-Vidéo. A rules engine in Odoo (cobra_price_rules) reads competitor prices delivered by Wiser several times a day and computes a target price — best valid competitor, bounded by a minimum margin. Two modes: observation (simulates) then live (applies and pushes to Shopify), with going live staying a human decision. Guardrails: never sell at a loss, outlier offers (< 60% of our price) ignored, pair/unit normalization. 6 families live, ~300 prices realigned; the rest adjusts on its own at each Wiser update.

  • 2026-08-07

    An app that reads supplier price lists for me

    Every supplier price list (a PDF or a “new product” email) meant manual work: read it, find the product in Odoo, check EAN/SKU, key in purchase and sale prices. An internal app (“Product Update & Creation”) automates the pipeline: PDF upload or watching a read-only mailbox, EAN/SKU/price extraction via a Claude model, matching against the Odoo database, duplicate detection before creation, systematic human validation and traceability. A deliberate guardrail: no created product is ever published online automatically.
