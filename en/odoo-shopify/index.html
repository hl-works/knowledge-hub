01 / WHY THESE TWO

Two strengths.
One business.

I want to improve the customer experience while keeping control of operations. This combination gives me two specialized systems and the freedom to build the business connections I need.

Odoo runs the operations.

Sales, purchasing, inventory, deliveries and invoices: an extensible business foundation. Community is its open-source edition; at Cobra, we use Enterprise on Odoo.sh.

Shopify powers commerce.

A platform built for selling: storefront, checkout, customer accounts and an app ecosystem. Its role in my architecture is to serve customers while Odoo organizes fulfillment.

↗

AI accelerates the work.

Reading code, mapping workflows, investigating discrepancies and preparing fixes. APIs and extensions give agents ways to interact. Permissions, tests and approvals determine what they can do.

05 / THE FDE APPROACH

Know the business.
Understand. Fix.

FDE means Forward Deployed Engineer: an engineer working alongside operations, turning a business problem into a solution that works in practice. This is the approach I take as a business leader: start with real work, investigate the system and verify the outcome with the team.

01

Understand the work

How the store sells, what logistics must prepare and what finance must substantiate. Useful rules start here.

02

Find the cause

Follow the order, data and events. Distinguish a code defect from an unclear business rule or missing information.

03

Fix and verify

Prepare a focused change, test it, deploy with a recovery plan and check the result in both systems.

For example, “paid” in Shopify is not sufficient proof that money was collected. Knowing the business means checking the transaction, invoice, payout and reconciliation before changing a status.

06 / AGENTS AROUND THE BUSINESS

Screens for the teams.
Agents to get work done.

Odoo and Shopify form the foundation. Around them, Cobra Apps makes operations visible and provides business tools. In Claude, connectors and skills let us request a search, analysis or preparation in natural language. Some capabilities reuse the same underlying services; each path has its own permissions and checks.

COBRA APPS

View, track, validate

Dashboards for orders, replenishment, supplier prices, invoice intake and integration monitoring.

CLAUDE + CONNECTORS / SKILLS

Ask, analyze, prepare

Find an email, investigate an invoice, prepare product records or propose a payment batch.

BUSINESS SERVICES

Check, execute, record

Scripts and services connected to Odoo, documents and email. Run on demand or on a schedule, depending on the workflow.

This diagram describes responsibilities, not a single gateway already covering every flow. Brain exposes selected capabilities with identity, permissions and an operation log; it does not automatically guard other connectors.

Supplier invoices

Retrieve attachments from email and purchase orders, extract lines, match them and prepare the bill in Odoo. A business screen supports invoice intake and validation.

Check the supplier, linked purchase order, lines, total and discrepancies.

Email reading and triage

Find context, organize messages, summarize requests and prepare replies or actions. Attachments become inputs to business workflows.

Reading or drafting does not authorize sending: the full message and recipients must be approved.

Products and supplier prices

Turn a supplier price list into proposed products and prices. Catalog matching comes before creation to identify duplicates and conflicts.

Creating a product in Odoo and publishing it on Shopify are separate decisions.

Orders and monitoring

Make pending orders, delayed imports and synchronization issues visible. Screens and alerts help locate the record and decide how to recover.

A monitor that flags an issue is not necessarily an agent that fixes it.

Recurring automation

Use scheduled services to retrieve documents, perform defined updates and run repeated checks. Claude helps investigate these routines and prepare changes.

Check triggers, permissions, run records and actual effects; code being present does not prove a job is enabled.

Finance and “batch out”

Group eligible supplier bills into a draft SEPA payment batch. The Claude skill reuses the existing business service to prepare the batch.

Bank details required, bills already in payment excluded, simulation first. Validation and bank transfer remain human actions.

Before you read

Is this an official Odoo or Shopify guide?

No. It’s an independent account of a real project, with official documentation references and a clear distinction between observed facts and planned improvements.

Is everything already automated at Cobra?

No. The book separates existing flows, deployed fixes and unfinished work. Invoice access, financial consistency and recovery paths still need end-to-end validation.

Is this combination right for every business?

It means maintaining two systems, their costs and an integration. A simpler business may only need one tool. This book helps you ask the right questions before choosing.
